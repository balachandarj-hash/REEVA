The clinical foundation every case deserves.

Our experienced nurses and medical-legal professionals deliver structured support at every stage of the patient journey.

01 — CINA™

Comprehensive Initial Nurse Assessment

A structured, head-to-toe assessment capturing condition, relevant history, functional limitations, medications, recovery barriers, and accident-related concerns.

02

Nurse Case Management

Ongoing clinical coordination and patient support that monitors progress, identifies barriers, supports adherence, and keeps recovery moving forward.

03

Legal Nurse Consulting

Medical-legal analysis that translates complex medical information into clear, actionable insight for personal-injury cases.

04

Medical Record Review

Structured review to identify clinical facts, treatment progression, missing documentation, inconsistencies, causation issues, and medical necessity concerns.

Life Care Planning Support

Clinical collaboration and longitudinal record development for more complete future-care analysis and evaluation of long-term needs.

06

Evidence-Based Perspective

A better way is being built

Infrastructure for the entire life of a case.

Personal-injury cases are often managed through disconnected offices, delayed records, manual follow-up, fragmented communication, and limited clinical visibility. REEVA is building the infrastructure to connect those moving parts.

The REEVA platform will bring together clinical oversight, appointments, medical records, case timelines, patient navigation, evidence-based review, treatment verification, utilization review, and stakeholder communication within one coordinated environment.
