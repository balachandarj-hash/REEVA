Clinical and medical-legal services for every stage of the case.

S-01 — CINA™

Comprehensive Initial Nurse Assessment

Comprehensive nurse baseline addressing injury history, symptoms, function, medications, care status, risks, barriers, and coordination priorities.

S-02

Legal Nurse Case Management

Ongoing nurse case monitoring, plan and activity records, status reporting, authorized provider communication, issue escalation, and follow-through.

S-03

Ongoing Care Coordination

Appointment, recommendation, record, referral, and barrier tracking with authorized communications among counsel, patient, providers, and other participants.

S-04

Medical Record Review & Chronologies

Record organization, clinical review, treatment timeline, chronology, summary, gap identification, and focused issue analysis.

S-05

Certified Medical Records Coordination

Record requests, authorization and certification coordination, status tracking, completeness review, organization, and secure delivery.

S-06

Medical-Legal Evidence Review & Quality Assurance

Focused review of mechanism, chronology, preexisting conditions, documentation consistency, treatment support, and evidentiary gaps for counsel.

S-07

Utilization / Treatment Review (UTR/UR)

Attorney-directed review of proposed or completed care for clinical support, documentation sufficiency, timing, sequencing, and guideline alignment.

S-09

Pre-Litigation Clinical Case Screening

Early clinical screening of records and proposed care to identify strengths, risks, documentation gaps, and questions before significant case investment.

S-10

Clinical Team Conferences & Status Reporting

Structured interdisciplinary conferences, issue lists, action tracking, recommendations for consideration, and periodic reports to authorized counsel.

S-11

Life Care Planning Support

Data collection, record development, clinical coordination, and qualified life-care-planning support as defined in the applicable module.

S-12

Vocational Rehabilitation Support

Functional and vocational information development, record support, coordination, and qualified vocational consulting when expressly retained.

S-13

Expert Witness, Deposition & Trial Support

Separate written retention for expert reports, affidavits, deposition, hearing, or trial services by an accepted qualified professional.

S-14

Case Visibility Platform & Practice Access

Authorized portal access for scheduling, case status, task tracking, records, communications, reporting, and other enabled tools.

S-15

Other Approved Service

A service expressly described in an incorporated module, Statement of Work, or written assignment authorization.

A better way is being built

Infrastructure for the entire life of a case.

Personal-injury cases are often managed through disconnected offices, delayed records, manual follow-up, fragmented communication, and limited clinical visibility. REVVA is building the infrastructure to connect those moving parts.

The REVVA platform will bring together clinical oversight, appointments, medical records, case timelines, patient navigation, evidence-based review, treatment verification, utilization review, and stakeholder communication within one coordinated environment.
